# Core domain concepts

A personal investment-portfolio tracker for retail investors: submit buy/sell limit orders, the broker fills them, tracks current holdings, exposes portfolio state as JSON. No UI. Single broker, single exchange (NYSE). Many investors, each with exactly one account; each account starts with a USD balance (0) and may open one extra balance in EUR. Trading goes through USD only: EUR can be opened and deposited into, but is never used to buy or sell and cannot be converted (the EUR fallback is the Course Module 2 lesson).

> Many-to-many between investors and accounts is possible in real life (joint accounts; one investor with several specialized accounts), but the demo models one investor per account.

Package layout: `domain` (shared: `Money`, `Currency`, `Symbol`, `Quote`), `domain.order`, `domain.portfolio`, `domain.account`.

- **`Investor`** (id, name) and **`Account`** (id, investor). The account holds no cash itself.
- **`Portfolio`** (accountId, `List<CashBalance>`, `List<Position>`) — the one piece of persisted, mutated state.
- **`CashBalance`** (amount, currency) — what a portfolio holds. **`Money`** (`BigDecimal` + `Currency`) — every other amount (quote price, limit price, fill price, cost basis). Never `double`. `Currency` is `USD` or `EUR`.
- **`Position`** (symbol, quantity, costBasis) — `costBasis` is the TOTAL cost of the position, USD. No stored value: market value and gain/loss are computed at read time from a live quote.
- **`OrderRequest`** (submitted) → **`Order`**, one of three outcomes: `FILLED` (`fillPrice`/`filledAt`, from the exchange), `REJECTED` (the broker refused to send it: `RejectionReason` `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` or `INSUFFICIENT_POSITION`), or `KILLED` (the exchange received it but did not fill it: `KillReason` `LIMIT_NOT_MET` or `UNKNOWN_SYMBOL`). Never persisted, no pending state. The exchange's answer is an `ExecutionReport` (`Filled` or `Killed`).
- **Order rules:** limit orders only; `timeInForce` is `FOK` only (optional, defaults to FOK, anything else → 400). The exchange decides: it fills the whole quantity at its price if BUY price ≤ limit or SELL price ≥ limit, otherwise it kills the order (`LIMIT_NOT_MET`; an unlisted symbol is killed too, `UNKNOWN_SYMBOL`). No partial fills, no resting orders. The broker takes the exchange's answer at face value (no sanity check of fills). Before sending, the broker rejects a SELL of more than the position holds (`OrderValidator`, `INSUFFICIENT_POSITION`, no shorting) and a BUY whose worst-case cost (`limitPrice × quantity` plus the flat fee) exceeds the USD balance (`BuyingPowerValidator#canAfford`, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`; exactly equal is fine, EUR is ignored). A SELL needs no funds check, because the fee comes out of its proceeds.
- **Broker fee:** a flat fee in USD (`broker.fee-usd` in `broker.properties`, 1.00 by default; 0 or negative means no fee) on every filled order. A buy pays it on top of the price. A sell has it taken out of the proceeds; if the proceeds are smaller than the fee, no fee is charged. Killed and refused orders cost nothing. EUR is never charged. Because the fee is not in the cost basis, `unrealizedGainLoss` ignores fees paid. Open question, left for the course: the FX conversion fee (currency and order).
- **Invariants live in the types:** a `CashBalance` is never negative; a `Position` always has at least one share and a cost basis above zero. Invalid ones cannot be constructed (`IllegalArgumentException`). `Money` may be negative (unrealized loss).
- **`applyFill(Portfolio, Order) → Portfolio`** (pure): BUY debits `fillPrice × quantity` plus the flat fee from USD (EUR is never touched) and adds to quantity and cost basis (the fee is not part of the cost basis). SELL credits USD with `fillPrice × quantity` minus the flat fee, or with the full proceeds when they are smaller than the fee (the broker waives it); it reduces quantity, reduces cost basis at average cost (`costBasis × remaining / old quantity`, `HALF_EVEN`, 2 decimals), and removes the position at zero. It checks nothing: the caller guarantees enough cash or shares, and the types refuse to build a negative result.

## Infrastructure adapters

- **`PortfolioStore`** (interface) + **`JdbcPortfolioStore`** (plain JDBC). Not a teaching focus; hidden from the learner. See `database.md`.
- **`StaticDataProvider`** (interface) + **`CsvStaticDataProvider`** — typed methods (e.g. `getFxRate(from, to)`), not a generic key-value lookup, even though more static data may be added later.
- **`ExchangeClient`** (`submitOrder`, `getQuotes`) + **`HttpExchangeClient`** (over `HttpClient`). Unit tests use hand-written fakes of the interface. The exchange's quote answer is mapped to the broker's own `Quote` (`Symbol`, USD `Money`, timestamp).
